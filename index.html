How to Connect Your Business with FBR PRAL Digital Invoicing

Complete walkthrough for shopkeepers, wholesalers, and importers under SRO 1852(I)/2025 & STGO No. 01 of 2026. Set up your digital sales tax terminal in under 5 minutes.

01 Prerequisite

Verify NTN & Sales Tax Registration

Ensure your business has an active 7-digit NTN (or 13-digit CNIC for sole proprietors) and is registered on the FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

  • Check ATL status via SMS ATL <CNIC> to 9966
  • Keep your STRN (Sales Tax Registration Number) ready
02 IRIS Portal

Obtain PRAL POS ID & Bearer Token

Log in to the official FBR IRIS e-portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk) to register your point-of-sale terminal and generate your digital API key.

  • Go to Digital Invoicing & POS Registration tab
  • Register Counter to receive POS ID (e.g. POS-01)
  • Generate Bearer Token for Sandbox (testing) or Production
03 Software Setup

Enter Credentials in SmartTax Pro

Open SmartTax Pro on your PC -> Click Universal Business Settings on the left sidebar.

  • Enter Business Name, Branch, Seller NTN, STRN & Address
  • Paste your PRAL Bearer Token and select Sandbox/Production
  • Click Test FBR Connection for instant green verification
04 Billing & POS

Issue Invoices with Verifiable QR Code

Click Smart Billing POS. Scan item barcode or choose items. SmartTax automatically applies 18% standard tax, further tax, or 3rd schedule rules.

  • Click Submit to FBR & Print
  • Real-time 22-digit FBR ID received in < 0.4 seconds
  • Print A4 Full Tax Slip or 80mm Thermal Receipt with QR Code
